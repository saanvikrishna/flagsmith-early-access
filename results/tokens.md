Generated by `python harness/bench.py --report` from [`results/bench/raw.jsonl`](bench/raw.jsonl) (25 live runs). Flagsmith Cloud + GitHub, `claude -p` headless, two-turn human approval.

Values are medians when a scenario ran more than once.
